About QR Signature

Create. Customize. Share.

What it is

QR Signature helps you make a QR code that looks like it belongs to you: your colors, your shapes, your logo. It then checks that the code still scans, and helps you share it.

What works today

  • Nine types of code, with plain-language checks on what you type.
  • Twelve templates, six dot patterns, three corner frames and two corner centers, solid or gradient colors, and your own logo or profile picture.
  • A scan check that explains what it finds.
  • PNG downloads at three sizes, SVG downloads, and copying the image.
  • Sharing to Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X, the phone share sheet, Instagram through an image, and a copy-link button.

What we promise

  • Privacy first. This version has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. What you type stays on your device.
  • Honesty. We do not show features that do not exist. Anything planned is labeled as planned.
  • Readable by everyone. The site works with a keyboard and a screen reader, and it respects reduced-motion settings.

What is next

QR Signature is being built in stages. This is the first one. Later stages are planned to add private file sharing, password-protected and expiring links, accounts, and codes you can edit after printing. We will not add any of them until the stage before has been tested and approved.
