Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This is a draft. It describes how the first version works. It is not legal advice, and it must be reviewed by a qualified person, and given a contact address, before the site is opened to the public.

The short version

QR Signature makes your code inside your own browser. The link, text, Wi-Fi password, contact details and logo you enter are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. This version has no accounts, no forms that send data, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no outside fonts. The fonts and icons are stored with the site.

What is stored on your device

When you press “Customize more” on the home page, the web address you typed is placed in your browser's temporary session storage so the Create page can pick it up. It is removed as soon as it is used, and it disappears when you close the tab. Nothing else is stored.

Sharing

The Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X buttons open those companies' own websites in a new tab, and they carry the link or message you chose to share. After that, their privacy policies apply, not ours. The phone share sheet hands the image to the app you pick. Copying a link or an image uses your device's clipboard.

Hosting

When this site is put on the internet, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as the time of a visit and the address it came from. We will name the host here before the site opens to the public.

If this changes

We will not add accounts, analytics or any other way of collecting information without first updating this page and saying so on the site.

Contact

A contact address will be added here before the site opens to the public.
